# Alleonzern Lore. Micro-note.
[[MULTIVERSE DIMENSION UTILIZATION LICENSE (M&K WORLD LICENSE)]]

This corporation started by creating fictional weaponry for a game called "Bruderbeschuss"... It manufactured various toy blasters and other play equipment, but was never involved in producing actual military inventory.

The corporation came to power relatively recently. Only 75 years ago. This was achieved with the help of the Psychowave Emitter, invented by Keone Mayatnikus. The device regulated neurotransmitters, inducing artificial surges of dopamine, serotonin, cortisol, norepinephrine, and similar substances. Conventmen (from the word Convent — the local equivalent of a parliament) who were loyal to the corporation would smuggle this device into assemblies and manipulate the decisions being made. They triggered dopamine and serotonin surges for any resolutions passed in favor of the future Alleonzern.

Consequently, a harmless manufacturer of toy blasters first began absorbing other corporations, and eventually ended up ruling the entire galaxy.

All the militarization we see is not a preparation for war. The Ellesmere galaxy is fully explored. No military threat to the corporation exists. It is merely a hobby and a fetish of the board of directors, who produce military hardware strictly for their private collections.

Following all this, Keone Mayatnikus sought protection under the last surviving corporation, which had repurposed itself into a mercenary business: Merc-4-more. Prior to this, they were engaged in manufacturing military equipment for a state that was once located in Ellesmere, about which almost nothing is known today. This corporation used to be small, as military conflicts in the galaxy were a distant, long-forgotten past. In fact, no information about them has even been preserved.

How it magically happened that peace and tranquility reigned in the galaxy is something I will come up with later. And even if I don't, it doesn't really matter. This information doesn't hold much critical value, though it will look highly presentable in excerpts, dialogue elements, and NPC folklore. Most likely, it will be devised together with the development team or will come to me on its own if the prompt slips into subconscious processes.

This explains the parity peace between Alleonzern and Merc-4-more. Since Merc-4-more manufactures real weapons and provides actual military services, they are, quite simply, not numerous enough to pose a serious threat. However, they are powerful enough that eliminating them would involve far too many high risks for the corporation.

That is why the mercenaries do not represent ordinary bandits. They are professional military personnel engaged in mercenary activities. Nevertheless, judging by everything, they didn't have much in the way of principles, considering they betrayed their previous employer as soon as Alleonzern offered them a more compelling agreement.

After Diana arrives in Ellesmere and hires Merc-4-more, the corporation, in fact, only then begins to actively arm itself and acquire an army. They did possess actual warships, such as the Baatvia and...

Their very first weaponry consisted of simple modifications of already existing toys, adapted for live ammunition. One such weapon, which features an advertisement, is the Ziemlichgenau.

Not all forms proved successful for real weaponry. Other modifications derived from toy armaments include the Dränger, the Mittlere grenade, and the Sehrgenau.

This dynamic applied to tanks and heavy weaponry as well. The corporation maintained police forces; however, they never suppressed major riots, meaning that conventional firearms were not required. More precisely, there was only one exception: the predecessor to the Mikrosplitter. The Mikrosplitter itself is that very modification of a police shotgun. The Makrosplitter, on the other hand, is a completely optimized version engineered specifically for live ammunition.

The predecessor to the Allesperre was merely a power shield utilized instead of glass in areas where increased security was required, yet quick access to the object was necessary in case of an emergency.

The Begleitends were originally a "temporary boost" in "Bruderbeschuss" that were later repurposed into permanent companions.

The exact same logic applies to the Hulse mortel. It was essentially a super-attack within that same toy game, which they managed to manifest into a fully functioning combat device.

The Fliessen is a cutting-edge development by scientists and was deployed for field testing.

The Caidoz falls into this category too. However, due to its excessive firepower, manufacturing complexity, and small magazine capacity, it serves more as a collector's item for the wealthy. In fact, it was recommended for destruction so that the technology would not fall into the hands of Merc-4-more.
