# Witya Peros

***Name Meaning***: From the word Witty. It was given to him by Katya from the word Witty, which from the language of its creators was translated as witty.
***Surname meaning:*** The surname comes from the pagan god Perun, due to the fact that Witya can "throw thunder and lightning" with incredible force and, in principle, is not much averse to a fight, but knows when to stop.
***Nick:*** Mechanic. That was his name before he met Katya. He was called the Mechanic because of his talent with mechanics, but he was also familiar with electronics.

# Katya

***Name Meaning:*** Witya mangled her first name so much because she kept trying to give him a name and didn't even understand what a name was. But it turned out that the names they gave each other stuck.
***Nick:*** Kit. She was intended as a kit of parameters for the Great Breguet, to add empathy to this script executor.

# Boris Hartocat

***Name and Surname Meaning:*** This character's first and last name have a monolithic foundation of meaning. Specifically, they derive from Boris the Razor. Or more precisely, from his second nickname, Boris Hard-to-hit. However, this character is extremely difficult to catch, and no prison can hold him—he constantly escapes. Therefore, his last name comes from the words "Hard-to-catch". He is hoarse and wheezes when speaking.

She's running under a Laryngoguise, constantly changing shapes, and storming PantoCorp bases? # Greig Ventre

***Name and Surname Meaning:***: The name and surname comes from J. Craig Venter, whose team created the world's first synthetic bacterium with entirely artificial, computer-generated DNA. Dr. Craig Ventre's team recommended the destruction of the experiment, Pantopet, at Pantocorp's Deep Space Disposal Facility (DSDF). He is the creator of D.Y.N.O. 2.

# Taisi Athina

***Name and Surname Meaning:*** Her name and surname come from Tais Athins (Taisia Athinos), the Muse and keeper of stories. She understands how fleeting power is and how eternal feelings are.
Taisia shares with her what she knows: stories from the underbelly of PantoCorp, tales of organics willing to sacrifice themselves for their loved ones, and how emotions break through any of Peter Koen's harsh, cold scripts and orders. This makes Katya's teaching sincere and "dirty," without pompous metaphysics. Katya pieces together the soul, piece by piece, from the real-life experiences of the underclass of Ellesmere.

# Remhen Rev

***Name and Surname Meaning:*** The word is formed by the reverse pronunciation and division of the German word "Wernehmer," which literally translates as "interrogator." He interrogates Vitya and Boris before offering them a contract. He is also the one who subsequently dumps Vitya in the middle of the desert. Apparently, his powers are not limited to interrogations, and he exercises a certain degree of control over the soldiers who signed the contract under an agreement that cannot be rejected.
